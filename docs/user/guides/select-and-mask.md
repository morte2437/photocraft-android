Pick a selection tool, drag around the area, then choose **Layer › Layer Mask › Reveal Selection** to show only that area. A mask hides part of a layer without erasing any pixels.

## Make a selection

Use one of these:

- **Elliptical Marquee Tool** or **Rectangular Marquee Tool** for simple shapes. Drag on the canvas.
- **Quick Selection Tool**, **Magic Wand Tool** or **Lasso Tool** to follow the edges of an object.
- **Select › Subject** to let PhotoCraft pick the main subject. The **Quick Selection Tool** and **Object Selection Tool** also have a **Select Subject** button in the options bar. If nothing is found, PhotoCraft says "No subject found" and suggests the Object Selection or Lasso tool.

Smart selection runs on your computer. It needs no account and no cloud.

## Adjust the selection

- **Select › Inverse Selection** swaps the selected and unselected areas.
- **Select › Modify** has **Border…**, **Smooth…**, **Expand…**, **Contract…** and **Feather…**. Feather softens the edge.
- **Select › Select and Mask…** refines an edge, for example around hair.
- **Select › Deselect** removes the selection. **Select › Reselect** brings back the last one.

## Turn the selection into a mask

1. Click the layer you want to mask in the **Layers** panel.
2. Choose **Layer › Layer Mask › Reveal Selection** to show only the selected area, or **Hide Selection** to hide it.

To start with the whole layer visible or hidden and paint the mask yourself, use **Layer › Layer Mask › Reveal All** or **Hide All**.

## Remove or apply a mask

- **Layer › Layer Mask › Delete** removes the mask.
- **Layer › Layer Mask › Apply** applies the mask to the layer's pixels.
- **Layer › Layer Mask › Disable Layer Mask** (or **Enable Layer Mask**) switches the mask off and on.

To limit a color adjustment to the selection, see [Adjust colors without changing your pixels](adjust-colors-with-adjustment-layers.md).
